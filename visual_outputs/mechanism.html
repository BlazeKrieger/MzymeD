📋 Catalytic Mechanism Explanation

Enzyme: Laminarinase (β-1,3-glucanase) from real crystal structure (PDB: 2W52)

Substrate: β-1,3-glucan (laminarin)

Mechanism: Double-displacement (retaining) glycosidase

Frame Breakdown:

  • Frames 1-9: Substrate approaching the enzyme active site (CYAN spheres move in)
  • Frames 10-19: Substrate entering active site (enzyme opens conformationally)
  • Frames 20-29: Michaelis complex formation (substrate bound, enzyme breathing)
  • Frames 30-36: Nucleophilic attack by GLU107 (RED) - C-N bond formation
  • Frames 37-43: ⚡ GLYCOSIDIC BOND CLEAVAGE - C-O bond breaks, covalent intermediate
  • Frames 44-50: Product release and enzyme regeneration

Key Catalytic Residues:

  • 🔴 GLU107 (Red) - Nucleophile; directly attacks C1 carbon of substrate
  • 🟠 ASP256 (Orange) - General acid; protonates the leaving group
  • 🟡 TRP257 (Yellow) - Substrate binding and stabilization
  • 🟣 GLY49 (Purple) - Active site loop forming binding pocket

References:

  • GH16 family: CAZy (Carbohydrate-Active enZYmes) database classification
  • Mechanism: Double displacement with covalent intermediate
  • Crystal structures: 2W52, 2W39, 4BPZ, 4BOW (RCSB PDB)
